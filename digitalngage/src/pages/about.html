Our story

Born to close the gap between marketing & tech.

We saw businesses juggling one agency for posts, another for ads, a freelancer for the website and spreadsheets for leads. Nothing talked to anything else — and leads slipped through the cracks.

So we built DigitalNgage as one team that does it all: the reels and the ads that create demand, the websites and stores that capture it, and the CRM software that makes sure every lead turns into a conversation.

Today we manage the complete digital operations for brands across manufacturing, organic foods, infrastructure, engineering, oil & gas and technology.
