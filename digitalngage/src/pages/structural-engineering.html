FAQ

Structural questions, answered.

What do you need to start a structural design?
The architectural drawings (plans, sections, elevations), the soil investigation report if available, the site location and how the building will be used. For industrial projects, also share crane, equipment and roofing requirements.
Do you design both steel and RCC structures?
Yes — steel, RCC, composite and pre-engineered (PEB) structures, including PUF panel roofing and cladding systems for sheds, warehouses and cold storage.
Do you provide only drawings, or design too?
Both. You can hire us for complete design and analysis with drawings, or only for 2D/3D detailing based on an existing design.
Which codes do you follow?
Indian Standards — including IS 456 (RCC), IS 800 (steel), IS 875 (loads), IS 1893 (earthquake) and IS 13920 (ductile detailing). Other international codes can be used when a project requires them.
Can you also market our construction or infrastructure business?
Yes. That's exactly what we do for Delta Infrastructures — structural engineering plus website, Meta Ads, SEO, social media and a sales CRM, all from one team.
