The challenge

Steel buildings are won on speed and trust.

01

Design fast, design right

Every PEB enquiry needs a structural scheme, steel tonnage and a price quickly — and the final design has to be safe for wind and seismic loads and economical enough to win the order.

02

Drawings the site can build

Fabrication and erection teams need clear GA, shop and erection drawings. Every unclear detail costs a site query, a delay or rework.

03

Buyers search before they call

Factory owners and developers look for PEB builders on Google and social media first. Without a strong website, ads and proof of past work, the enquiry goes to a competitor.

The solution

One team from enquiry to site.

Step 2CRMLogged, assigned, followed up

Step 5Drawings2D/3D detailing for site

Outcome

Engineering and growth, in one place.

Delta works with one partner for the structure and for the sales engine. Enquiries from the website and ads land in the shared Metro CRM, the engineering team prepares the scheme and drawings, and finished projects go back onto the website and social media as proof for the next buyer.

The result is a portfolio of 20 completed projects across Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Goa — from 8,000 sq.ft sports courts to 1,30,000 sq.ft warehouses.
