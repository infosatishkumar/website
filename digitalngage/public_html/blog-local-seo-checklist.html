The local SEO checklist for businesses in India.

On this page
Google Business Profile Reviews Consistent name, address & phone Your website Track what matters
Share

When someone searches "PUF panel manufacturer near me" or "organic honey in Bengaluru", Google shows a map with three businesses before any website. Getting into that map pack is often the fastest SEO win a local business can get.

Google Business Profile

  • Claim and verify your profile with the exact business name.
  • Choose the most specific primary category, then add secondary ones.
  • Add real photos — your shop, factory, team and products — every month.
  • Fill in services and products with prices where possible.
  • Post updates and offers weekly; it signals an active business.

Reviews

  • Ask every happy customer for a review with a short direct link.
  • Reply to every review — good or bad — within a couple of days.
  • Never buy reviews; Google removes them and can suspend profiles.

Consistent name, address & phone

Your name, address and phone number should be identical on your website, Google, JustDial, IndiaMART, Facebook and every directory. Small differences confuse Google about which listing is real.

Your website

  • Put your city and service in page titles and headings naturally.
  • Create a page for each main service and each city you serve.
  • Embed a Google Map and add LocalBusiness schema.
  • Make sure the site loads fast on mobile data.

Track what matters

Watch calls, direction requests and website clicks in your Business Profile insights, and Search Console impressions for local keywords. Rankings are a means — enquiries are the goal.
