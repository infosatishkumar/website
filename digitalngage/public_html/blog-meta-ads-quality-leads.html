7 ways to get quality leads (not junk) from Meta Ads.

On this page
1. Switch the form to "Higher intent" 2. Ask one qualifying question 3. Say the price range in the ad 4. Call within 5 minutes 5. Feed results back to Meta 6. Retarget before you prospect harder 7. Judge campaigns on cost per sale
Share

A ₹40 lead that never picks up the phone costs more than a ₹200 lead that buys. When we run Facebook and Instagram campaigns for manufacturers and D2C brands, we optimise for one thing: leads your sales team actually wants to call. Here is how.

1. Switch the form to "Higher intent"

Meta's Instant Forms have a "More volume" and a "Higher intent" option. Higher intent adds a review screen before submitting, which removes accidental taps. Volume drops a little; quality jumps a lot.

2. Ask one qualifying question

Add a short multiple-choice question that a real buyer can answer instantly — "Which product do you need?", "Quantity required?" or "When do you plan to buy?". It filters out the curious and gives your sales team context before the first call.

3. Say the price range in the ad

If your minimum order is ₹50,000, say "Bulk orders from ₹50,000" in the creative. You will get fewer leads, but nobody wastes a call discovering they can't afford you.

4. Call within 5 minutes

Lead quality drops sharply with every hour you wait. We connect forms to WhatsApp alerts and a CRM so the right salesperson is notified instantly — the same setup behind Metro CRM, which handles 6,000+ leads.

5. Feed results back to Meta

Mark which leads became customers and send that signal back through the Conversions API. Meta's algorithm then finds more people like your buyers, not just more form-fillers.

6. Retarget before you prospect harder

Website visitors, video viewers and Instagram engagers already know you. Retargeting them with a testimonial or a factory video is usually the cheapest source of serious enquiries.

7. Judge campaigns on cost per sale

Cost per lead is a vanity number. Track cost per qualified lead and cost per sale, and move budget towards the ad sets that produce revenue.
