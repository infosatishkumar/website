The challenge

Great products, but no easy way to buy them online.

KIO makes raw honey, wood-pressed oils and desi ghee. The brand needed a website that looks as premium as the jars on the shelf — and a checkout that Indian shoppers trust, without extra work for the owner.

01

Manual UPI payments

Customers scanned a QR code and typed their transaction ID by hand. Every payment had to be matched against the bank statement.

02

Mobile-first buyers

Most customers shop on phones and expect to pay with the UPI app they already use.

03

Order follow-up

The owner needed full order details — customer, products, payment — in one place, the moment an order is placed.

Storefront

A warm, natural look that matches the brand.

Deep forest green, warm cream and honey-gold, with a classic serif for headings. Every product card shows sizes, price with MRP, key highlights and two clear actions.

  • Shop by categoryHoney, Oil and Ghee menus list every product, plus search and an "All Products" page.
  • Sizes and prices on the cardSwitching between ½ Ltr and 1 Ltr updates the price instantly.
  • Add to Cart or Buy NowBuy Now skips the cart and goes straight to checkout.
Product pages

Everything a careful buyer wants to know.

An image gallery, size and quantity selectors, and tabs for description, ingredients, storage and FAQ. Related products keep people browsing.

  • Clear delivery promise"Ships in 2–5 business days, Pan India" sits right under the buy buttons.
  • Easy to updateAll products and prices live in one data file — change a price without touching the design.
Cart

A cart that does the maths for you.

Change quantities with + and −; pressing − on the last item removes it. The summary shows subtotal, shipping (free above ₹599), 5% tax and the final total.

Checkout & payments

Pay the way you already pay.

Two short steps. First, delivery details. Then every payment option is listed with its own icon — the Pay button glides under the option you pick and shows the exact amount.

Under the hood

How an online payment is confirmed.

Payments run through Razorpay. The website never trusts the browser for money: the server works out the price, and the payment is checked twice before the order is marked paid.

Step 1Customer taps PayThe cart is sent to the server — product and size only, no prices.
Step 2Server prices itPrices come from the server's product list, so nobody can change the amount.
Step 3Razorpay opensThe chosen method opens first — UPI app, card, bank or wallet.
Step 4Signature checkThe server verifies Razorpay's secure signature.
Step 5Double-checkThe server asks Razorpay to confirm the payment and exact amount.
Step 6Order confirmedMarked paid; emails go to the customer and the owner.

Keys stay on the server

The Razorpay secret key is only used in server code and never reaches the browser.

No double processing

If the same payment is confirmed twice, the order is updated and emailed only once.

Backup confirmation

A Razorpay webhook confirms payments even if the customer closes the page too early.
