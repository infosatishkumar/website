FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How soon will I see results?
Paid ads (Meta & Google) usually start bringing leads within the first 7–14 days. SEO is a longer game — most clients see meaningful ranking movement in 3–6 months. Social media growth builds steadily month on month.
Do you work with small businesses and startups?
Yes. We work with startups, D2C brands, manufacturers and B2B companies. Packages are flexible — start with one service and add more as you grow.
Is the ad budget included in your fee?
No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta or Google from your own ad account, so you keep full ownership and transparency. Our fee covers strategy, creatives, management and reporting.
Can you build a website or online store and also market it?
That is our speciality. We built and run the KIO Organics store end-to-end — design, development, payments, social media and ads — so every part works together.
Can you build custom software like a CRM?
Yes. We built Metro CRM, which manages 6,000+ leads from Facebook, Google, IndiaMART and the website, with quotations, job cards, payments and an AI assistant. We can build a tool around how your team works too.
Do I need to sign a long-term contract?
No. We work month to month. We would rather earn your business with results every month than lock you in.
